\section*{Selected Projects}
\begingroup\small

\project{CampusBay: Student Marketplace}{2026}{Live marketplace where students list items or post requests and close deals on WhatsApp. Next.js 16 on Cloudflare Workers with D1 and Drizzle, instant updates and search in English, Malay and Indonesian. Built and run solo.}

\project{UMOVE: Campus Food Delivery}{2026}{Food from any UM cafe, delivered by verified UM students and paid on arrival. React 19, Hono and PostgreSQL, with a live request board, push notifications and Telegram alerts.}

\project{Spektra: Study Reminders for UM}{2026}{Pulls SPeCTRUM deadlines, classes and announcements automatically and reminds students on Telegram, email and push. FastAPI and SQLite, installable as a PWA and Android app.}

\project{Morsel: Surplus Campus Food}{2026}{WIX2002 team project: campus cafes post unsold food at up to half price and students reserve it with a pickup code. Next.js, Supabase and Tailwind, with live listings and seller approval.}

\project{PaperMind: Local RAG Paper Analyzer}{2026}{Ask questions of any PDF with answers grounded in the text. FastAPI, ChromaDB and Ollama, fully offline and self-hosted.}

\project{AI Digit Recognizer: CNN From Scratch}{2026}{Convolutional network written entirely in NumPy, from convolutions to backpropagation and Adam. 99.1\% on the MNIST test set, with a layer-by-layer visualizer.}

\project{Nalar: Blog with a Custom CMS}{2026}{Node.js, Express and PostgreSQL blog with JWT auth, Markdown editing and scheduled publishing.}

\project{Crop Disease Detector}{2026}{Classifies 38 plant diseases from a leaf photo with MobileNetV2 transfer learning in TensorFlow/Keras, about 97\% accuracy, on Hugging Face Spaces.}

\endgroup
