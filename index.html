Hi there! I'm Rafi

I'm a Computer Science (Software Engineering) student at the University of Malaya, focused on software engineering and full-stack development. I got into programming through web development back in school, and I keep coding because I love building systems that are useful and solve real problems.

I serve in the Website Division of PEKOM's Technical Executive Committee at Universiti Malaya, working on the society's web platforms and technical operations. At PPI Malaysia I served as Head of Website Division, leading the team that built and maintained the official PPI Malaysia website. I was also Head of Department for the IDFEST Art Exhibition (PPI University of Malaya) — running it end-to-end to showcase Indonesian culture internationally.

Outside of code, I enjoy sharing knowledge, explaining technical topics in a simple way, and building things that are meaningful and practical.
